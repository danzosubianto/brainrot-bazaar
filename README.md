**Brainrot Bazaar** is a 3D Roblox brainrot tycoon with a cast of fourteen absurd, entirely original characters — Kevin the Damp Potato, Madame Waffleiron, Baron von Bubblewrap, Granny Turbo-Scooter, The Sentient Wet Sock, and GREG (just Greg, a completely normal man). Brainrots step out of the Portal of Questionable Decisions onto the Summoning Sidewalk, one at a time, wearing their name, rarity and money-per-second on a label. Buy one and it follows you home on foot; step on your colour-coded plot to place it on a pedestal, where it earns into your vault. Step on the gold vault pad to collect.

The climb: six rarities (Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic → BRAINROT GOD), rolled mutations (Shiny, Upside-Down, Made of Beans), four upgrade lines at the Obelisk of Upgrades (walk speed, carry capacity, extra pedestals, a longer lock), and rebirths that burn the whole bazaar down for a permanent +50% income multiplier each.

The threats: **the Janitor** does his rounds and sweeps the richest unlocked base's best brainrot; **the Boulder of Responsibility** rolls down Main Street and bonks whatever you are still carrying; and rival players can lift a brainrot off any unlocked foreign pedestal. The lock button — and the dash home — answer all three.

Built as a Rojo project: every script is a `.luau` file under `src/` (shared cast/config in ReplicatedStorage, one-job server services, a ScreenGui client), and `game.rbxl` is built from them. To play it, open the built place in Roblox Studio and press Play, or publish it and play it on Roblox.

## Run it

```sh
rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
